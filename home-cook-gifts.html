Questions people ask

What do you get someone who loves to cook?

Reliable basics they'll use constantly: a cast iron skillet, an instant-read thermometer, a good knife set or a multi-cooker.

What is a good gift for a beginner cook?

A cutting board set, a filled spice rack or a compact air fryer. They make cooking easier without needing much skill.

What is a splurge gift for a home cook?

An espresso machine, an air fryer or an Instant Pot. They're bigger-ticket items that get used for years.
