Questions people ask

What do you get someone who is always cold?

A heated throw blanket, fuzzy slippers, rechargeable hand warmers, a plush robe or a weighted heating pad.

Are heated blankets a good gift?

Yes. They're one of the most-used winter gifts, especially for people who feel cold at home in the evenings.

What is a small gift for someone who is always cold?

Fuzzy slipper socks or rechargeable hand warmers make great stocking stuffers.
