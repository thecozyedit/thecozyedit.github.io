Questions people ask

What do you buy a homebody?

Comfort upgrades: a weighted blanket, a book light, a puzzle, a tea sampler or a cozy lounge set. Think of things that make a quiet night in feel special.

Is a weighted blanket a good gift?

For most people, yes. It's one of the most-loved comfort gifts of recent years, especially for people who like to unwind on the couch or have trouble settling down at night.

What is a good gift for an introvert?

Something they can enjoy alone at home, like a Kindle, a 1000-piece puzzle, a sunrise alarm clock or a calming tea set.
