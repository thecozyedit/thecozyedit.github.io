Questions people ask

What is a good housewarming gift?

A charcuterie board, a reed diffuser, a hand soap and lotion set or an olive oil dispenser are easy, crowd-pleasing choices.

What should you bring to a housewarming party?

Something useful for the new space, or something for entertaining, like a serving board or a hosting gadget. See our First Apartment Essentials guide for more.

How much should I spend on a housewarming gift?

There's no set rule. A thoughtful small gift is always appropriate, and closer friends and family often give a bigger home item.
