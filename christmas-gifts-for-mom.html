Questions people ask

What is a good Christmas gift for mom?

A WiFi digital photo frame, a mulberry silk pillowcase, a plush robe or a candle warmer lamp. They're thoughtful, useful and easy to love.

What is a sentimental Christmas gift for mom?

A digital photo frame the whole family can send pictures to from their phones. Load it with old family photos before you wrap it.

What do you get a mom who has everything for Christmas?

Small luxuries she won't buy herself, like a heated neck massager, a jewelry box or a cozy tea and cocoa set. Our Gifts for Mom Who Has Everything guide has more ideas.
