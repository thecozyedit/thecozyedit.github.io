Questions people ask

What are good stocking stuffers for women?

Lip masks, hand-care sets, pimple patches, cocoa bombs, fuzzy socks and travel-size beauty kits are reliable favorites.

How many stocking stuffers should I get?

Most people do 4–8 small items. Mix something practical, something sweet and something a little indulgent.

Can these be gift-exchange gifts?

Yes. Many of these work well for Secret Santa, white elephant or coworker gifts.
