Questions people ask

What do you need for a first apartment?

Kitchen basics like a knife set, cutting boards, a cast iron skillet and a filled spice rack, plus a few things that make it feel like home, like a diffuser or pillow covers.

What is a good gift for someone moving into their first apartment?

A compact air fryer, an Instant Pot or a knife set. Practical, bigger items that help them cook at home.

What are cheap first apartment essentials?

Faux succulents, a reed diffuser, an olive oil dispenser or a hand soap set. They're small things that make a space feel finished.
