Questions people ask

What is a good cozy gift for her?

A plush throw blanket, a fleece robe or a silk pillowcase are reliable choices. They're useful, feel indulgent and suit almost anyone.

What can I get a woman who has everything?

Go for small luxuries she wouldn't buy herself, like a mulberry silk pillowcase, a candle warmer lamp or a weighted heating pad. Our Gifts for Mom Who Has Everything guide has more ideas.

Are these gifts good for Christmas?

Yes. Every pick works for Christmas, birthdays or a just-because gift, and most ship quickly with Prime.
