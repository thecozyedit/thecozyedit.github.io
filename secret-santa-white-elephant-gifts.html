Questions people ask

What is a good Secret Santa gift for anyone?

Hot cocoa bombs, a mug warmer, fuzzy socks, rechargeable hand warmers or a holiday mug. They're universally liked and easy to wrap.

What makes a good white elephant gift?

Something fun that people actually want, like a mini waffle maker, an electric wine opener or a 1000-piece puzzle. Useful-but-fun gifts get stolen the most.

What is a good Secret Santa gift for a coworker?

Keep it neutral and useful: an insulated tumbler, a mug warmer, a hand cream set or a candle gift set.
